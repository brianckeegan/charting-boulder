- Elementary grades also showed an upward trend beginning in fall 1983. From fall 1972 to fall 1991 elementary grades reflected a 13.4 percent increase.
- Membership in secondary grades declined overall 11.0 percent from fall 1972 to fall 1989 with a slight rise in 1975 and 1976. From fall 1989 to fall 1991 secondary grade membership has shown a slight increase.
