

# INTRODUCTION

This report has been prepared from information collected from Colorado's public school districts.

Many student membership reports contained in this publication are similar to those we have published in prior years. In addition to providing high school graduates by district, this report includes graduation rates for the Class of 1991. The dropout rate included in Table 2 is for grades 10, 11 and 12 only. Additional information on dropout rates and graduation rates is available in a separate publication titled Colorado Public School Annual Dropout Rates - Grades Seven Through Twelve 1990-91 and Class of 1991 Graduation Rates.

# HIGHLIGHTS:

- In the fall of 1991 there were 593,030 students in Colorado's public schools. This is the second consecutive year Colorado's membership has shown a substantial increase from the previous year. The fall 1990 membership increased by 11,458 students (2.0%) and the fall 1991 membership increased by 18,817 students (3.3%).
- The fall 1991 membership was the largest in Colorado's history, surpassing the previous membership peak in 1972. Colorado's public school membership declined 5.6 percent between 1972 and 1983 before beginning an upward trend. Between 1983 and 1991 fall membership increased by 23.8 percent for minority students, 5.3 percent for white students and 9.4 percent overall.
- Native Americans, Asians, Blacks and Hispanics represented 25.1 percent of the total student population, while these same groups made up 9.3 percent of Colorado's teachers.
- Fall 1991 membership in elementary grades increased 3.7 percent over fall 1990 and 21.6 percent over fall 1983. However, membership in secondary grades decreased 4.2 percent from fall 1983 and increased 2.6 percent over fall 1990.
- The pupil/teacher ratio in Colorado for fall 1991 was 17.9 to 1. However, when special education and special subject teachers were excluded, the ratio became 23.9 to 1.
- The Denver Metropolitan Area includes 319,385 (53.9 percent) of the total state's student membership. Colorado's ten largest school districts, with student membership over 17,000, account for 327,064 (55.2%) of the state's total student membership.
- The graduation rate for Class of 1991 was 78.9 percent. The decline of 1.1 percentage points from the 1990 graduation rate of 80.0 percent is a result of the 1990 rate being based on a 3-year rate encompassing grade 10-12. The Class of 1991 graduation rate is based on a 4-year rate. A 3-year recalculation for the Class of 1991 produced an 80.5 percent graduation rate.
