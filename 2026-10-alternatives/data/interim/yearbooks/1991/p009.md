Bar chart showing state trends in fall membership by grade range from 1986 to 1991. The Y-axis represents membership in thousands, ranging from 0 to 160. The X-axis shows four grade ranges: GRADES 1-3, GRADES 4-6, GRADES 7-9, and GRADES 10-12. For each range, there are six bars representing the years 1986, 1987, 1988, 1989, 1990, and 1991. Membership generally increases in the lower grade ranges and decreases in the higher grade ranges over the period.

During the period from fall 1986 to fall 1991 membership increased by 11.7 percent in grades 1-3, 19.0 percent in grades 4-6 and 5.7 percent in grades 7-9, while grades 10-12 decreased by 10.2 percent. The current trend not only indicates increasing student membership in the lower grades but also the progression of the elementary increase into the secondary grades.
