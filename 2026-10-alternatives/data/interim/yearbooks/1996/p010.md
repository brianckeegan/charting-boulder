## STATE TRENDS IN STUDENT MEMBERSHIP FROM FALL 1972 TO FALL 1996

This line graph illustrates the total student membership in Colorado from Fall 1972 to Fall 1996. The vertical axis (Enrollment) ranges from 520,000 to 680,000 in increments of 20,000. The horizontal axis (Year) shows years from 72 to 96 in two-year increments. The data shows a peak in Fall 1972 at 574,248, a decline to a low point around 1983, and a subsequent sharp increase to a current membership of 673,438 in Fall 1996.

This line graph compares enrollment in elementary and secondary grades from Fall 1983 to Fall 1996. The vertical axis (Enrollment) ranges from 220,000 to 400,000 in increments of 20,000. The horizontal axis (Year) shows years from 83 to 95 in two-year increments. Elementary grades (solid line) show a steady increase from approximately 285,000 in 1983 to over 380,000 in 1996. Secondary grades (dashed line) show a decline from approximately 258,000 in 1983 to a low of about 240,000 in 1989, followed by a sharp increase to nearly 295,000 in 1996.

Line graph showing Elementary and Secondary Grades enrollment from 1983 to 1996. The y-axis represents Enrollment from 220,000 to 400,000. The x-axis represents the Year from 83 to 95. Elementary grades are shown with a solid line, and secondary grades with a dashed line.

- Colorado's public school membership declined 5.6 percent between 1972 and 1983 before beginning an upward trend. Student membership for the last four years surpassed the previous membership peak in fall 1972. Between 1983 and 1996 fall membership increased 24.2 percent overall.
- Elementary grades reflected a 6.7 percent decline from fall 1972 to fall 1983. Beginning fall 1983 elementary grades experienced a large increase in membership. From fall 1983 to fall 1996 student population in elementary grades increased by 96,598 students (33.8%). The fall 1996 student membership in elementary grades increased by 7,326 students from fall 1995.
- Membership in secondary grades declined overall 11.7 percent from fall 1972 to fall 1989 with a slight rise between 1973 and 1976. From fall 1989 to fall 1996 secondary grade membership has shown a 23.0 percent increase. Student membership for secondary grades in fall 1996 increased by 9,833 students over the fall 1995 membership and was the largest since fall 1977.
