# MISSION STATEMENT

The Research and Evaluation Unit of the Colorado Department of Education empowers our coworkers, the public, and the educational community to make informed decisions regarding quality education for Colorado's diverse learners by collecting and disseminating accurate, timely information, and by sharing our expertise in data collection, research and evaluation.
