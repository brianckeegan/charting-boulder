Bar chart titled 'Membership Trends Ten Largest / All Other Districts' showing student membership in thousands from 1986 to 1996 for the ten largest districts and all other districts.

- From fall 1986 to fall 1996 Colorado's public school membership increased by 115,023 students (20.6%). Colorado's ten largest school districts in 1996 increased their membership by 56,203 students (18.0%) during that same period. The ten largest districts account for 368,514 (54.7%) of the state's total fall 1996 public school membership.
- The Denver/Metro area includes 371,705 (53.7%) of the state's total fall 1996 student membership in public schools. From fall 1986 to fall 1996, student membership in the Denver/Metro area increased by 61,152 students (20.3%). The total increase for all other Colorado school districts during that period was 53,871 students (20.4%).
