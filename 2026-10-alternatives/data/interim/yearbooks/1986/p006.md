

# INTRODUCTION

This report has been prepared from information collected from Colorado's public school districts.

Many of the reports are similar to those we have published in prior years. However, we have added other reports which we feel will be informative. Included in these additions is a report by school district providing the annual dropout rate from grades 10 - 12 by ethnic/racial group and by sex. The district rate reflects dropout data for the traditional high schools. Those districts having an alternative high school for dropouts and/or potential dropouts are identified in the report with an asterisk. The dropout rate for all the alternative high schools is reported before the state rate. There are many other districts which offer alternative programs for their dropout students but their counts are reported as part of their traditional high school. The state rate includes all dropouts from grades 10 - 12.

Also included is a summary table of the dropout rates beginning with the 1974-75 school year and a table of graduation rates by ethnic/racial group.
