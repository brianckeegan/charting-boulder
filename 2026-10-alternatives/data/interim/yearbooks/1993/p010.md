Line graph showing Elementary and Secondary Grades membership in thousands from 1972 to 1993. Elementary grades show a general upward trend after 1983, while secondary grades show a decline until 1989 followed by a slight rise.

- Colorado's public school membership declined 5.6 percent between 1972 and 1983 before beginning an upward trend. The fall 1991, fall 1992 and fall 1993 student membership surpassed the previous membership peak in fall 1972. Between 1983 and 1993 fall membership increased 15.3 percent overall.
- Elementary grades reflected a 6.7 percent decline from fall 1972 to fall 1983. Beginning fall 1983 elementary grades experienced a large increase in membership. From fall 1983 to fall 1993 student population in elementary grades increased by 76,867 students (26.9%). The fall 1993 student membership in elementary grades increased by 4,612 students from fall 1992. This represented the smallest annual growth since 1984.
- Membership in secondary grades declined overall 11.7 percent from fall 1972 to fall 1989 with a slight rise between 1973 and 1976. From fall 1989 to fall 1993 secondary grade membership has shown a 10.9 percent increase. Student membership for secondary grades in fall 1993 was the largest since fall 1978.
