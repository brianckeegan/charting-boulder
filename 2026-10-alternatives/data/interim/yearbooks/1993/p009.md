Bar chart titled 'Membership Trends Ten Largest / All Other Districts' showing membership in thousands from 1983 to 1993. The chart compares 'TEN LARGEST' districts (solid bars) and 'ALL OTHER DISTRICTS' (hatched bars). Membership for both groups shows a steady increase over the decade, with the ten largest districts consistently having higher membership than all other districts combined.

- From fall 1983 to fall 1993 Colorado's public school membership increased by 82,866 students (15.3%). Fall membership in the ten largest school districts increased by 37,314 students (12.3%) during that same period. Colorado's ten largest school districts account for 341,466 (54.6%) of the state's total fall 1993 public school membership.
- The Denver/Metro area includes 336,007 (53.8%) of the state's total fall 1993 student membership in public schools. From fall 1983 to fall 1993, student membership in the Denver/Metro area increased by 43,374 students (14.8%). The total increase for all other Colorado school districts during that period was 39,492 students (15.8%).
