

# INTRODUCTION

This report has been prepared from information collected from Colorado's public school districts.

Many of the reports are similar to those we have published in prior years with the exception of the dropout data. The dropout rate included in Table 2 is for grades 10, 11, and 12 only. Additional information is available in a separate publication titled "Report on Colorado Annual Dropout Rates - Grades Seven through Twelve 1988-89."

# HIGHLIGHTS:

- . In the fall of 1989 there were 562,755 students enrolled in Colorado's public schools. This represented an increase from the 1988 fall membership of 560,081.
- . Membership in grades Kindergarten through Sixth increased 2.1 percent over Fall 1988 and 12.1 percent over Fall 1980.
- . Colorado's public school membership declined 4.7 percent between 1975 and 1983 before beginning an upward trend and showing a 3.8 percent increase between 1983 and 1989.
- . Minority student enrollment increased by 14.2 percent between 1983 and 1989 and accounted for 24.3 percent of school membership in 1989.
- . The pupil/teacher ratio in Colorado for fall 1989 was 17.6 to 1. However, when special education and special subject teachers were excluded, the ratio became 23.3 to 1.
- . The Denver Metropolitan Area includes 301,946 (53.7 percent) of the total state's pupil membership.
