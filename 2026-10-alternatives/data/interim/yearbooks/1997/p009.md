- From fall 1987 to fall 1997 Colorado's public school membership increased by 126,931 students (22.7%). Colorado's ten largest school districts in 1997 increased their membership by 66,352 students (21.3%) during that same period. The ten largest districts account for 377,909 (55.0%) of the state's total fall 1997 public school membership.
- The Denver/Metro area includes 371,327 (54.0%) of the state's total fall 1997 student membership in public schools. From fall 1987 to fall 1997, student membership in the Denver/Metro area increased by 70,260 students (23.3%). The total increase for all other Colorado school districts during that period was 56,671 students (21.9%).
