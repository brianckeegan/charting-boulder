- Colorado's public school membership declined 5.6 percent between 1972 and 1983 before beginning an upward trend. Student membership since 1991 has consistently surpassed the previous membership peak in fall 1972. Between 1983 and 1997 fall membership increased 26.7 percent overall.
- Elementary grades reflected a 6.7 percent decline from fall 1972 to fall 1983. Beginning fall 1983 elementary grades experienced a large increase in membership. From fall 1983 to fall 1997 student population in elementary grades increased by 102,471 students (35.9%). The fall 1997 student membership in elementary grades increased by 5,873 students from fall 1996.
- Membership in secondary grades declined overall 11.7 percent from fall 1972 to fall 1989 with a slight rise between 1973 and 1976. From fall 1989 to fall 1997 secondary grade membership has shown a 19.1 percent increase. Student membership for secondary grades in fall 1997 increased by 7,856 students over the fall 1996 membership.
