Bar chart titled 'Membership Trends Ten Largest / All Other Districts' showing membership in thousands from 1983 to 1994. The chart compares the 'TEN LARGEST' districts (solid black bars) with 'ALL OTHER DISTRICTS' (hatched bars). Membership for both groups shows a steady upward trend over the 12-year period. The 'TEN LARGEST' group starts at approximately 295,000 in 1983 and reaches about 350,000 in 1994. 'ALL OTHER DISTRICTS' starts at approximately 250,000 in 1983 and reaches about 290,000 in 1994.

- From fall 1983 to fall 1994 Colorado's public school membership increased by 98,325 students (18.1%). Colorado's ten largest school districts in 1994 increased their membership by 56,237 students (19.2%) during that same period. The ten largest districts account for 349,101 (54.5%) of the state's total fall 1994 public school membership.
- The Denver/Metro area includes 342,787 (53.5%) of the state's total fall 1994 student membership in public schools. From fall 1983 to fall 1994, student membership in the Denver/Metro area increased by 50,154 students (17.1%). The total increase for all other Colorado school districts during that period was 48,171 students (19.3%).
