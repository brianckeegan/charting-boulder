Line graph showing Elementary and Secondary Grades membership in thousands from 1972 to 1994. Elementary grades show a decline until 1983 followed by a sharp increase. Secondary grades show a general decline until 1989 followed by an increase.

- Colorado's public school membership declined 5.6 percent between 1972 and 1983 before beginning an upward trend. Student membership for the last four years surpassed the previous membership peak in fall 1972. Between 1983 and 1994 fall membership increased 18.1 percent overall.
- Elementary grades reflected a 6.7 percent decline from fall 1972 to fall 1983. Beginning fall 1983 elementary grades experienced a large increase in membership. From fall 1983 to fall 1994 student population in elementary grades increased by 83,159 students (29.1%). The fall 1994 student membership in elementary grades increased by 6,292 students from fall 1993.
- Membership in secondary grades declined overall 11.7 percent from fall 1972 to fall 1989 with a slight rise between 1973 and 1976. From fall 1989 to fall 1994 secondary grade membership has shown a 14.8 percent increase. Student membership for secondary grades in fall 1994 increased by 9,167 students over the fall 1993 membership and was the largest since fall 1977.
