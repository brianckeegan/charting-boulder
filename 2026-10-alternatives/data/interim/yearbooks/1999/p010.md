- Colorado's public school membership declined 5.6 percent between 1972 and 1983 before beginning an upward trend. Student membership since 1990 has consistently surpassed the previous membership peak in fall 1972. Fall membership increased 30.6 percent overall between 1983 (542,196) and 1999 (708,109).
- Elementary grades reflected a 6.7 percent decline from fall 1972 to fall 1983. Beginning fall 1983 elementary grades experienced a large increase in membership. From fall 1983 to fall 1999 student population in elementary grades increased by 111,027 students (38.9%). The fall 1999 student membership in elementary grades increased by 3,804 students from fall 1998.
- Membership in secondary grades declined overall 11.7 percent from fall 1972 to fall 1989 with a slight rise between 1973 and 1976. From fall 1989 to fall 1999 secondary grade membership has shown a 31.5 percent increase. Student membership for secondary grades in fall 1999 increased by 5,170 students over the fall 1998 membership.
