Bar chart showing state trends in fall membership from 1986 to 1990 for four grade groups: Grades 1-3, Grades 4-6, Grades 7-9, and Grades 10-12. The y-axis represents thousands of students, ranging from 0 to 160. The x-axis shows the grade groups. For each group, there are five bars representing the years 1986 (solid black), 1987 (diagonal lines), 1988 (dotted), 1989 (cross-hatch), and 1990 (horizontal lines). Membership generally shows an upward trend for Grades 1-3 and a downward trend for Grades 10-12.

During the period from Fall 1986 to Fall 1990 membership increased by 9.4 percent in grades 1-3, 13.3 percent in grades 4-6 and 1.1 percent in grades 7-9, while grades 10-12 decreased by 11.7 percent.

Should this trend continue, the decade of the nineties will not only show increasing pupil membership in the lower grades but will also show the progression of the elementary increase into the secondary grades.
