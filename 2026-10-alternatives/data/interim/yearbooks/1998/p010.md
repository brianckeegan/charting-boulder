Line graph showing Elementary and Secondary Grades enrollment in Colorado public schools from Fall 1983 to Fall 1998. The Y-axis represents Enrollment from 220,000 to 400,000. The X-axis represents the Year from 1983 to 1998. Elementary Grades (solid line) show a steady increase from ~285,000 in 1983 to ~395,000 in 1998. Secondary Grades (dashed line) show a decline from ~258,000 in 1983 to ~238,000 in 1989, followed by a steady increase to ~308,000 in 1998.

- Colorado's public school membership declined 5.6 percent between 1972 and 1983 before beginning an upward trend. Student membership since 1990 has consistently surpassed the previous membership peak in fall 1972. Between 1983 and 1998 fall membership increased 28.9 percent overall.
- Elementary grades reflected a 6.7 percent decline from fall 1972 to fall 1983. Beginning fall 1983 elementary grades experienced a large increase in membership. From fall 1983 to fall 1998 student population in elementary grades increased by 107,223 students (37.8%). The fall 1998 student membership in elementary grades increased by 4,752 students from fall 1997.
- Membership in secondary grades declined overall 11.7 percent from fall 1972 to fall 1989 with a slight rise between 1973 and 1976. From fall 1989 to fall 1998 secondary grade membership has shown a 19.1 percent increase. Student membership for secondary grades in fall 1998 increased by 7,216 students over the fall 1997 membership.
