Two series are shown: 'Ten Largest Districts' (solid black bars) and 'All Other Districts' (hatched bars). Both series show a steady upward trend over the decade.

- From fall 1988 to fall 1998 Colorado's public school membership increased by 139,054 students (24.8%). Colorado's ten largest school districts in 1998 increased their membership by 82,611 students (27.2%) during that same period. The ten largest districts account for 386,155 (55.2%) of the state's total fall 1998 public school membership.
- The Denver/Metro area includes 380,233 (54.4%) of the state's total fall 1998 student membership in public schools. From fall 1988 to fall 1998, student membership in the Denver/Metro area increased by 79,511 students (26.4%). The total increase for all other Colorado school districts during that period was 59,543 students (22.9%).
