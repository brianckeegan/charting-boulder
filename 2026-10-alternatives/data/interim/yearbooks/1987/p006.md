

# INTRODUCTION

This report has been prepared from information collected from Colorado's public school districts.

Many of the reports are similar to those we have published in prior years with the exception of the dropout data. The dropout rate included in Table 1 is for grades 10, 11, and 12 only. Additional information is available in a separate publication titled "Report on Colorado Annual Dropout Rates - Grades Seven through Twelve 1986-87."

# HIGHLIGHTS:

- . In the fall of 1987 there were 560,236 students enrolled in Colorado's public schools. This represented 0.3 percent more than the 1986 fall membership of 558,415.
- . Grades one through three reflected the largest increase in student enrollment; 139,199 for fall 1987 over 134,088 for fall 1986, a 3.8 percent increase.
- . Colorado's public school membership declined 4.7 percent between 1975 and 1983 before beginning an upward trend and showing a 3.3 percent increase between 1983 and 1987.
- . Minority student enrollment increased by 13.2 percent between 1977 and 1987 and accounted for 23.4 percent of school membership in 1987.
- . The pupil/teacher ratio in Colorado for fall 1987 was 18.0 to 1. However, when special education and special subject teachers were excluded, the ratio became 23.7 to 1.
- . The 1987 pupil/teacher ratios by type of school indicated a higher ratio for elementary schools (20.2) than for middle schools (16.7), junior high schools (18.9), or senior high schools (17.9).
