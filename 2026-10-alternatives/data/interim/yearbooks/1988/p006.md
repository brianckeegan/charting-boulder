

# INTRODUCTION

This report has been prepared from information collected from Colorado's public school districts.

Many of the reports are similar to those we have published in prior years with the exception of the dropout data. The dropout rate included in Table 2 is for grades 10, 11, and 12 only. Additional information is available in a separate publication titled "Report on Colorado Annual Dropout Rates - Grades Seven through Twelve 1987-88."

# HIGHLIGHTS:

- . In the fall of 1988 there were 560,081 students enrolled in Colorado's public schools. This represented a slight decrease from the 1987 fall membership of 560,236.
- . Grades two through four reflected the largest increase in student enrollment; 135,588 for fall 1988 over 130,988 for fall 1987, a 3.5 percent increase.
- . Colorado's public school membership declined 4.7 percent between 1975 and 1983 before beginning an upward trend and showing a 3.3 percent increase between 1983 and 1988.
- . Minority student enrollment increased by 15.8 percent between 1978 and 1988 and accounted for 24.0 percent of school membership in 1988.
- . The pupil/teacher ratio in Colorado for fall 1988 was 17.8 to 1. However, when special education and special subject teachers were excluded, the ratio became 23.6 to 1.
- . The 1988 pupil/teacher ratios by type of school indicated a higher ratio for elementary schools (20.0) than for middle schools (17.2), junior high schools (18.6), or senior high schools (17.3).
