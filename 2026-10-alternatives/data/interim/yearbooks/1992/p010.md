Line graph showing Elementary and Secondary Grades membership in thousands from 1972 to 1992. Elementary grades show a decline until 1983 followed by a sharp increase. Secondary grades show a general decline until 1989 followed by a slight rise.

- The fall 1991 and fall 1992 student membership surpassed the previous membership peak in fall 1972. Colorado's public school membership declined 5.6 percent between 1972 and 1983 before beginning an upward trend. Between 1983 and 1992 fall membership increased 13.0 percent overall.
- Elementary grades reflected a 6.7 percent decline from fall 1972 to fall 1983. Beginning fall 1983 elementary grades experienced a large increase in membership. From fall 1983 to fall 1992 student population in elementary grades increased by 72,255 students (25.3%).
- Membership in secondary grades declined overall 11.0 percent from fall 1972 to fall 1989 with a slight rise between 1973 and 1976. From fall 1989 to fall 1992 secondary grade membership has shown a 7.6 percent increase.
