Bar chart showing state trends in fall membership by grade range from 1988 to 1992. The Y-axis represents membership in thousands, ranging from 0 to 160. The X-axis shows four grade ranges: GRADES 1-3, GRADES 4-6, GRADES 7-9, and GRADES 10-12. For each range, there are five bars representing the years 1988, 1989, 1990, 1991, and 1992. Membership generally increases over time for all grade ranges, with the most significant growth in the GRADES 4-6 and GRADES 7-9 categories.

During the period from fall 1988 to fall 1992 membership increased by 7.5 percent in grades 1-3, 19.3 percent in grades 4-6 and 14.0 percent in grades 7-9, while grades 10-12 decreased by 0.9 percent.

The current trend not only indicates increasing student membership in the lower grades but also the progression of the larger elementary membership into the secondary grades.

Public school membership has shown a substantial increase both at the elementary and secondary grade levels as school-age students migrate into Colorado.
