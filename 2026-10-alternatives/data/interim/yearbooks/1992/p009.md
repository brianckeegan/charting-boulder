Bar chart titled 'Membership Trends Ten Largest / All Other Districts' showing membership in thousands from 1983 to 1992. The chart compares the 'TEN LARGEST' districts (solid black bars) with 'ALL OTHER DISTRICTS' (hatched bars). Membership for the ten largest districts shows a steady increase from approximately 305,000 in 1983 to 337,000 in 1992. Membership for all other districts also increases, from about 240,000 in 1983 to 278,000 in 1992.

- From fall 1983 to fall 1992 Colorado's public school membership increased by 70,439 students (13%). Fall membership in the ten largest school districts increased by 33,310 students (11%) during that same period. Colorado's ten largest school districts account for 337,462 (55.1%) of the state's total fall 1992 public school membership.
- The Denver/Metro area includes 330,248 (53.9%) of the state's total fall 1992 student membership in public schools. From fall 1983 to fall 1992, student membership in the Denver/Metro area increased by 37,615 students (13%).
