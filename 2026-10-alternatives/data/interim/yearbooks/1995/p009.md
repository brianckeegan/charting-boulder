Bar chart titled 'Membership Trends Ten Largest / All Other Districts' showing membership in thousands from 1985 to 1995 for the ten largest districts and all other districts. The chart shows a steady increase for both groups over the decade.

- From fall 1985 to fall 1995 Colorado's public school membership increased by 105,637 students (19.1%). Colorado's ten largest school districts in 1995 increased their membership by 49,153 students (15.9%) during that same period. The ten largest districts account for 358,181 (54.5%) of the state's total fall 1995 public school membership.
- The Denver/Metro area includes 351,621 (53.5%) of the state's total fall 1995 student membership in public schools. From fall 1985 to fall 1995, student membership in the Denver/Metro area increased by 54,160 students (18.2%). The total increase for all other Colorado school districts during that period was 51,477 students (20.3%).
