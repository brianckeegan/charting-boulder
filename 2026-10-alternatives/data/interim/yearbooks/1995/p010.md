Line graph showing Elementary and Secondary Grades membership in thousands from 1983 to 1995. Elementary grades show a steady increase, while secondary grades show a decline followed by a rise.

- Colorado's public school membership declined 5.6 percent between 1972 and 1983 before beginning an upward trend. Student membership for the last four years surpassed the previous membership peak in fall 1972. Between 1983 and 1995 fall membership increased 21.0 percent overall.
- Elementary grades reflected a 6.7 percent decline from fall 1972 to fall 1983. Beginning fall 1983 elementary grades experienced a large increase in membership. From fall 1983 to fall 1995 student population in elementary grades increased by 89,272 students (31.2%). The fall 1995 student membership in elementary grades increased by 6,113 students from fall 1994.
- Membership in secondary grades declined overall 11.7 percent from fall 1972 to fall 1989 with a slight rise between 1973 and 1976. From fall 1989 to fall 1995 secondary grade membership has shown a 19.8 percent increase. Student membership for secondary grades in fall 1995 increased by 9,645 students over the fall 1994 membership and was the largest since fall 1977.
